# Drovy — Coming Soon Video Script

**Product:** Drovy  
**Format:** Simple promo (30–45 seconds)  
**Style:** On-screen text + voiceover  
**Tone:** Friendly, clear, confident  

---

## Overview

| Section        | Duration  | Purpose                          |
|----------------|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Hook           | 0:00–0:05 | Grab attention                   |
| Problem        | 0:05–0:15 | Show the pain                    |
| Solution       | 0:15–0:25 | Introduce Drovy                  |
| How it helps   | 0:25–0:35 | Owner + driver benefits          |
| Coming soon    | 0:35–0:45 | Close with launch message        |

**Total length:** ~40–45 seconds  

---

## Scene-by-scene script

### Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:05)

**Visual:** Simple motion graphic or business/delivery b-roll  

**On-screen text:**
```
Running a delivery business?
```

**Voiceover:**
> Running a delivery business is not easy.

---

### Scene 2 — Problem (0:05–0:15)

**Visual:** Quick cuts — phone ringing, paper notes, confused team  

**On-screen text (show one line at a time):**
```
The problem

Orders on phone calls

Paper notes & confusion

Drivers miss updates
```

**Voiceover:**
> Orders come through calls and WhatsApp. Details get lost on paper. Owners don’t know what’s pending. Drivers don’t always know what to deliver next. It’s messy and stressful.

---

### Scene 3 — Solution (0:15–0:25)

**Visual:** Drovy logo + app UI mockups (blur if not ready)  

**On-screen text:**
```
The solution

Drovy

One app for everyone
```

**Voiceover:**
> Drovy fixes this. One app where owners create and track orders, and drivers see only their assigned deliveries — clear and updated in real time.

---

### Scene 4 — How it helps (0:25–0:35)

**Visual:** Split or sequential — owner screen, then driver screen  

**On-screen text:**
```
For owners
Create orders • Track payments • Manage customers

For drivers
See assignments • Update status • Deliver on time
```

**Voiceover:**
> Owners stay in control. Drivers stay focused. No more confusion — everyone works from the same system.

---

### Scene 5 — Coming soon (0:35–0:45)

**Visual:** Logo on brand background  

**On-screen text:**
```
Drovy

Coming soon

Stay tuned
```

**Voiceover:**
> Drovy is launching soon. Simple delivery management for your business. Stay tuned.

---

## Production notes

- **Pace:** Slow and clear (~110 words per minute)
- **Music:** Light, upbeat background track (low volume under voice)
- **Voice:** One speaker, neutral accent, warm tone
- **Captions:** Add subtitles for social media (many viewers watch muted)
- **Brand color:** Use Drovy primary brand colors on text overlays and end card
